How to interpret the experiment

This is a fictional ecosystem with explicitly invented physiological equations. Fasting in this model means zero food intake, not zero water. Traits evolve through survival and reproduction; strength/endurance also undergo short-term within-life adaptation. Results demonstrate what follows from the chosen assumptions, not what would happen to humans, specific animal species, or people repeating a 7-day fast. Compare scenarios, test assumptions and treat predictions as hypotheses.
